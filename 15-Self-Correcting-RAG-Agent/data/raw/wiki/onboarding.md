# New Hire Onboarding

Every new employee gets a laptop preloaded with the standard image on their first day. IT sets
up your Okta single sign-on account before you arrive, so you should be able to log into email,
Slack, and the internal wiki immediately.

Your first week checklist: meet your manager, complete the compliance training module, request
access to your team's shared drive, and set up direct deposit in the payroll system. Most teams
also schedule a buddy for your first two weeks to answer day-to-day questions.

If your laptop has not arrived by your start date, contact the IT helpdesk directly rather than
waiting -- provisioning delays are common around hiring pushes.
