# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may submit reimbursement requests for reasonable business expenses through the
finance portal. Any single expense over twenty-five dollars requires an itemized receipt --
a credit card statement line alone is not sufficient documentation.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to a daily per-diem limit that varies by city;
check the finance portal's rate table before booking. Alcohol is only reimbursable when it is
part of a client dinner, and must be itemized separately from the meal cost.

Reimbursement requests submitted more than ninety days after the expense date are denied by
default; exceptions require director-level sign-off.
